# Forms of comparative and superlative

Enlace: [[Comparative and superlative.pdf#page=2&selection=0,0,0,25|Comparative and superlative, página 2]].

1. Adjetives with only one syllable.
	1. Comparative: we add "-er".
		1. Cold: Colder.
		2. Small: Smaller.
	2. Superlative: we add "-est".
		1. Cold: Coldest.
		2. Small: Smallest.
	3. Exceptions.
		1. If we have consonant, vowel and consonante, we need to **double** the last consonant.
			1. Wet: Wetter.
			2. Big: Biggest.
		2. If the adjetive ends with "-y", this often change it to "i".
			1. Dry: Driest.
		3. If the adjetive ends with "-e", we don't need to add another "e", only an "r".
			1. Nice: Nicer.
2. Adjetives with two syllables.
	1. We use "more" or "most".
		1. Careful: More careful / most careful.
		2. Normal: More normal / most normal.
3. Adjetives with more than two syllables.
	1. Comparative: we use "more".
		1. Beautiful: More beautiful.
		2. Interesting: More interesting.
	2. Superlative: we use "most".
		1. Intelligent: Most intelligent.
		2. Expensive: Most expensive.
