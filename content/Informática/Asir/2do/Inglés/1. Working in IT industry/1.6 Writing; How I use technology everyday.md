# Introduction (20 - 30 words)

All days I wake up because my clock sounds, this is a perfect example of technology, but, it isn't alone: time is a form of technology. Previously to the Modern Age, people count the time using the sun. Who is the owner of the time? The technology is in all sites.

# Body (70 - 90)

I think that the actual relation of humanity with technology is dual: people use technology and technology use people. When my friends use TikTok in class, TikTok controls the mind of my friends. The algorithms choose what my fiends will see, choosing, then, that can think («_territorialization_»). And the technology of class distribution provides to the teacher, like in the panoptic, see all the students, and punish distracted friends. Moreover, I use technology, for example, to complete this writing: words, classification of color, wires, processor, funs... All of this are technologies.

# Conclusion (10 - 30)

Technology is in the air, in all places, in all times, in all actions. Making the life easier and difficult, interesting and repetitive, free and controlled. The correct balance needs a global cosmovision of the technique.
