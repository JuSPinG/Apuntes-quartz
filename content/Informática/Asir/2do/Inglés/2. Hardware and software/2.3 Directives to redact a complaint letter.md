We redact a complaint letter when are not satisfied with service provided. And normaly is redacted with normal forms.

# Main structure

1. Salutation: "Dear + {Sir / Madam} / Manager}".
	1. If the name is knowed, is recomended attach it with the correct formal form ("Dear Mr President Trump").
	2. Phrases recomended.
		1. I am writting to express my disappointment with.
		2. I would like to lodge a formal complaint against your company for the reasons outlined below.
2. Introducction: Indentify what the issue is.
	1. Attach important information that is belived important, and tell about the situation.
3. Body.
	1. 3 paragraphs and justifications.
	2. Include time, location and people to describle the problem.
	3. Phrases recomended.
		1. First of all.
		2. On top of that.
		3. As if that was not enough.
		4. To top it all off.
4. Closing paragraph.
	1. Suggest action to be taken.
	2. Is recommended say bye with: "Yours faithfully + \n + {name}".
	3. Phrases recomended.
		1. I strongly recommend that your organisation.
		2. It would be advisable to.
