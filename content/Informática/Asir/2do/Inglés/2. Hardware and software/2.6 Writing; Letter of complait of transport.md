Dear representative of Railway Company,

I redact this letter to express my absolut disconforment with the service provided in your train. I recently travelled to work by trainr, and the experience was a real nigthmare, more scaring than a bad movie with low budget.

First of all, the train was very dirty. The floor looks like a biological experiment and the seats had stains that I prefer not imagine what was it. In addition, the canteen facilities were poor. The food looks tired and sad, like it also did not want to be there. I ask to myself if someone of the direction eat in this place or only normal humans like me.

Moreover, due to works in the tracks, the train failed to arrive at its destination in time. Nobody informed nothing. Because of this disaster, I arrived late to my work and my boss was not happy. I still feel the shame in my soul.

Last, but not the least, I paid a ticket for a service, not for a survival experience. I lost time, dignity and probably some health.

I strongly recommend to the Railway Company clean the trains, improve the canteen, and compensate me for the lost time and moral damages. Otherwise, I will think seriously in never use again this trainr and tell all the people my terrible history.

Yours faithfully,

A very disappointed customer
