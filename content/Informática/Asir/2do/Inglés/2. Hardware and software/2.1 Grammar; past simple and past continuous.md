# _Past simple_

1. Uso.
	1. Cosas sucedidas en el pasado y ya acabadas.
2. Forma.
	1. Positiva: {sujeto} + ({verbo irregular en pasado} / {verbo en forma base} + "ed").
		1. Ejemplo: _I made drugs_.
		2. Ejemplo: _I played football_.
	2. Negativa: {sujeto} + "didn't" + {verbo en forma base}.
		1. Ejemplo: _I didn't play football_.
	3. Interrogativa: "Did" + {sujeto} + {verbo en forma base} - "?"
		1. Ejemplo: _Did you come with me?_
		2. Excepción: Es posible encontrarse con una "_wh-word_", cuando eso sucede, se añade al principio y ya.
			1. Ejemplo: _Where did you find him?_
3. Excepción: Las _spelling rules_ marcan cómo añadir "-ed".
	1. {verbo en infinitivo} + "{ed} / {d}".
		1. Visit: Visited.
		2. Love: Loved.
	2. ***
4. Ejercicios.
	1. The IT department ||installed|| (install) the new software last week.
	2. She ||didn't understand|| (not understand) the instruccions for the new system.
	3. ||Did he fix|| (he fix) the server issiues yesterday?
	4. They ||developped|| (develop) a new application for mobile devices.
	5. We ||didn't use|| (not use) that old computer for the project.
	6. ||Did you attend||(you attend) the IT conference last month?
	7. The team  ||created|| (create)a backup of all important files.
	8. He ||didn't recived|| (not recived) the email about the systemd update.
	9. (they upgrade) the network last weekend.
	10. The technician (test) the new hardware thoroughtly.

# _Past continuous_

1. Uso.
	1. Para tratar temas que están sucediendo en el momento.
2. Forma.
	1. Positiva: {sujeto} + {forma _to be_} + {verbo en presente continuo}.
		1. Ejemplo: _I am cooking_.
	2. Negativa: {sujeto} + {forma _to be_} + "not" + {verbo en presente continuo}.
		1. Ejemplo: _You are not arriving now_.
	3. Interrogativa: {forma _to be_} + {sujeto} + {verbo en presente continuo} - "?".
		1. Ejemplo: _Are you kidding me?_
3. Palabras clave.
	1. _At the moment_: Por el momento.
	2. _Currently_: Actualmente.
	3. _Listen!_: ¡Escucha!.
	4. _Look!_: ¡Mira!.
	5. _Now_: Ahora.
	6. _Right now_: Ahora mismo.
	7. _Today_: Hoy.
	8. _This morning / afternoon / evening_: Esta mañana / tarde / noche.
	9. _While_: Mientras.

# Ejercicios de práctica

1. I / (work) right now: ||I am working right now||.
2. She / (eat) breakfast every morning: ||She eats breakfast every morning||.
3. They / (not / watch) TV at the moment: ||They are not watching TV at the moment||.
4. Do / you / (like) pizza?: ||Do you like pizza?||.
5. He / (study) for exams these days: ||He is studying for exams these days||.
6. We / (not / go) to the gym on Sundays: ||We don't go to the gym on Sundays||.
7. Tom / (play) football every Friday: ||Tom plays football every Friday||.
8. Why / she / (cry)?: ||Why is she crying?||.
9. The sun / (rise) in the east: ||The sun rises in the east||.
10. I / (not / listen) to music now: ||I am not listening to music now||.
11. She / (watch) TV every evening?: ||Does she watch TV every evening?||.
12. We / (always / forget) our keys: ||We always forget our keys||.
