# Introduction (20 - 30 words)

Close the eyes, imagine a primitive world of humans which communities not pass of 62 in population. This little societies were very common in Palaeolithic and Neolithic. Probably, the first technology was the manage of fire or the tool. Without this technologies, any human can survive, so this writing isn't conduct to a scenario without technology.
# Body (70 - 90)

The technique born to serve to the human, we can define it like: "the technology is the act and matter that use humanity to produce changes with efficiency". So, according to the text, the totally destruction of technology arrives the humanity to death, including me. Now, the question have an immediately response: no, we can't survive, and then, I need to change the theme of writing: what means the partial destruction of technique.

The technique increase the products that a worker can produce in the same time, but, it have a secret price: the worker is now alienated. All the work is disposed of the final product, and the social factor of the work is disappear. These are techniques of social control, and I think that there are very bad for the people.

# Conclusion (10 - 30)

Not all technologies are bad, but, in 2025 it looks like it. The generative AI, the addictive social media, modern publicity, social algorithms... All of this sounds bad. In spite of this, I reaffirm my position: some technologies, like the train, are good for society, it depends of the intension of the use.
